## 🎨 Next-Gen Image Creation & Multimodal Diffusion
- **ChatGPT Images 2.5 (OpenAI)**: OpenAI’s upgraded image generation model natively integrated into ChatGPT, delivering photorealistic textures, complex multi-subject scene composition, and fine-grained localized prompt adherence.
- **Muse Spark 1.3 (Meta Research)**: Meta’s creative diffusion foundation model optimized for rapid design exploration, interactive aesthetic steering, and high-fidelity concept prototyping.
- **Intern Lumina U2 (InternLM / Shanghai AI Lab)**: A unified multi-codebook diffusion large language model spanning omni-visual understanding, text-to-image synthesis, and conversational image editing across images, video, and 3D.
- **LLaDA Image (Inclusion AI)**: A hybrid diffusion and masked generative architecture extending Large Language Diffusion Models into high-resolution visual generation and comprehension.

## 🎬 Video Generation & Character Motion
- **EditVid (PLAN Lab - UIUC)**: A unified training-free framework extending frozen MM-DiT image editors to temporally consistent, instruction-guided and subject-guided video editing without requiring video-specific retraining.
- **Video DeltaNet (UC Berkeley / Impossible Inc / UT Austin)**: A near-lossless hybrid attention architecture combining local sliding-window softmax and bidirectional linear memory. Enables MiniMax H3 to synthesize 14.4-second 768p video in just 11.23 seconds on 8 NVIDIA B200 GPUs.
- **Viggle Animate**: Viggle’s open controllable character animation engine on Hugging Face, enabling seamless motion transfer and character-consistent dynamic re-targeting.
- 🔗 [EditVid Project Page](https://plan-lab.github.io/projects/editvid/)
- 🔗 [Video DeltaNet (OpenVDN) Project Page](https://openvdn.github.io/)
- 🤗 [Viggle Animate on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/Viggle/Viggle-Animate)

## 🌍 Large World Models & 3D Spatial Systems
- **Atlas (World Labs)**: World Labs’ pioneering Large World Model (LWM) developed by Fei-Fei Li's team, capable of generating persistent, physically interactive 3D virtual worlds from single images or text prompts.
- **GWM Worlds 2 (Runway)**: Runway’s second-generation General World Model, offering controllable camera exploration, physics-consistent environmental dynamics, and long-horizon scene stability.
- **H3-World**: A language-to-world control framework that bridges semantic comprehension with continuous 6-DoF camera and character movement across complex visual scenes.
- **SolarWM**: An open-data, scalable training initiative for long-horizon video world models, addressing drift and temporal collapse across extended generative sequences.
- **WorldSculpt (Alaya Lab)**: A compositional 3D world generator that turns grounded video sequences into complete, individually placed, editable object meshes via multi-view generative priors.
- **UniMate (Princeton / UC Berkeley / MIT)**: A unified foundation model (SIGGRAPH Asia 2026) for generating articulated motion for arbitrary skeletons from rigged 3D assets and text prompts without per-skeleton retraining.
- 📖 [World Labs Atlas Announcement](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/atlas)
