## 🔥 Overview
The AI landscape surges forward this week with landmark announcements across frontier language models, persistent background agents, photorealistic video generation, and space-based computing. OpenAI unveiled **GPT-6.1 Sol** alongside **OpenAI Dots**—always-on proactive personal agents equipped with their own cloud environments—while Anthropic delivered **Claude Sonnet 5.5** with 30%+ speedups and lower task costs. On the creative and physical intelligence fronts, **Ideogram 4.5** brings localized surgical image editing, **Kling 4.0** extends cinematic video to native 30-second generations, **FLUX 3 Action** pioneers robotics action prediction, and Google initiates **Project Suncatcher** to test TPU clusters in orbit.

## 🧠 Frontier Models & Autonomous Agents
- **GPT-6.1 Sol (OpenAI)**: OpenAI’s upgraded enterprise workhorse model featuring a 1,050,000 token context window, 128K output tokens, and multi-tier reasoning effort controls (low to max) delivering near-Astra capabilities at high operational efficiency.
- **Claude Sonnet 5.5 (Anthropic)**: The faster, 30%+ more cost-effective successor to Sonnet 5. Leads Terminal-Bench 4.0 agentic coding benchmarks while introducing updated cybersecurity and biosecurity guardrails.
- **OpenAI Dots**: Always-on proactive AI agents living within the ChatGPT ecosystem. Operating on dedicated cloud computers with browser access, Dots autonomously conduct multi-step research and integrate across 4,000+ workplace applications.
- **Grok 4.7 (xAI)**: xAI's scaled-up base architecture engineered for complex codebases and high-throughput enterprise reasoning with aggressive cost-performance ratios.
- **Step 5 Preview (StepFun)**: A 600-billion parameter sparse Mixture-of-Experts (27B active) multimodal foundation model supporting a 1M token context window across text, vision, and video.

## 🖼️ Generative Image & Precision Editing
- **Ideogram 4.5**: Ideogram’s localized surgical image editing model. Enables targeted prompt-guided modifications without disturbing surrounding composition or degrading image resolution across multi-turn sessions.
- **Supra 2 Image (SupraLabs)**: An ultra-compact ~100M parameter Diffusion Transformer (DiT) text-to-image model trained entirely from scratch on synthetic data in under 10 hours on a single NVIDIA H100.
- 🔗 [Ideogram 4.5 Model Page](https://ideogram.ai/models/4.5)
- 🤗 [Supra 2 Image on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/SupraLabs/Supra2-IMG)

## 🎬 Advanced Video Synthesis & Refinement
- **Kling 4.0 & Kling 4.0 Flash (Kuaishou)**: The next generation of Kling’s cinematic video engine, supporting native 30-second generations, 10 keyframe anchors, 10-bit HDR 4K output, 21:9 anamorphic framing, and Omni Reference control.
- **SoL-Refiner (NVIDIA NVLabs)**: A "Speed-of-Light" single-step video refinement model that upscales low-resolution diffusion outputs to crisp 2K/4K resolution with minimal latency overhead.
- 🔗 [NVIDIA SoL-Refiner Project Page](https://nvlabs.github.io/Sana/Sol-Refiner/)

- **Gemini 3.8 Flash-TTS (Google)**: Google’s prompt-directed speech models (Flash TTS & Flash-Lite TTS), enabling natural-language stylistic direction and voice replication from just 30 seconds of reference audio.
- **ElevenLabs v4 & v4 Turbo**: A redesigned acoustic architecture featuring natural-language "audio tags" for emotional nuance, whisper control, and high-fidelity vocal delivery across 90+ languages.

## 🌍 World Modeling, 3D Scenes & Robotics
- **WorldCrafter (Peking Univ & Tencent ARC Lab)**: A camera-controllable autoregressive video world model leveraging implicit 3D-aware memory for long-horizon temporal and spatial consistency.
- **FLUX 3 Action (Black Forest Labs)**: A 7B parameter open-weight World Action Model for robotics, mapping visual observations to real-time physical actions and leading the NVIDIA RoboLab-120 benchmark (42.92%).
- **GAE (Geometry-Native World Generation)**: A generative framework learning a geometry-native latent space to produce 3D-consistent virtual worlds with aligned RGB, depth, and surface normal geometry.
- **Mira Scene (Vast Eden)**: A compositional single-image 3D scene reconstruction pipeline using Canonical Coordinate Maps (CCM) and diffusion transformers to extract fully editable 3D objects and spatial layouts.
- **U1 Humanoid (UBTECH)**: The UWORLD U1 ultra-bionic companion robot line, combining 88 degrees of freedom, realistic silicone skin, and localized privacy-centric emotional AI.
- 🔗 [WorldCrafter Project Page](https://drexubery.github.io/WorldCrafter/)
- 📖 [Black Forest Labs FLUX 3 Action](https://bfl.ai/models/flux-3-action)
- 🔗 [GAE Geometry World Generation](https://jiah-cloud.github.io/GAE.github.io/)
- 🔗 [Mira Scene Project Page](https://vast-eden.ai/?p=mira-scene)

## 🔬 Extreme Efficiency, Specialized Reranking & Space Infrastructure
- **Limite 1B Violetto (Paradigma)**: A 1-billion parameter dense autoregressive model supporting 131K context, specifically tuned for high-throughput competition mathematical reasoning with ~2GB VRAM requirements.
- **CLM (Contrastive Language Model - 8B)**: An action-scoring and reranking checkpoint utilizing a frozen Qwen encoder to evaluate candidate decisions rapidly without text generation overhead.
- **Dots3 Note (Xiaohongshu)**: An open-source 280B Mixture-of-Experts (16B active) multimodal model under Apache 2.0 with a 512K context window for long-horizon agentic task execution.
- **Project Suncatcher (Google Research)**: Google’s orbital moonshot to test solar-powered satellite constellations equipped with Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) and free-space optical links for off-planet AI infrastructure.
