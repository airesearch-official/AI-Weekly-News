## 🧠 Frontier Reasoning, Vision & On-Device AI Hardware
- **Qwen 4 / Qwen 3.8 Flash Next (Alibaba)**: Alibaba Qwen's latest architectural preview, introducing next-generation streaming reasoning, ultra-low latency inference, expanded tool-calling autonomy, and unified multimodal capabilities.
- **Apple M6 & M5 Pro (Mac Mini AI PCs)**: Apple's newly announced Mac Mini lineup powered by the M6 and M5 Pro chips. Featuring massively upgraded Neural Engines and high-bandwidth unified memory, these machines are built to run persistent local multi-agent workflows and on-device generative models with peak efficiency.
- **DeepSeek V4 / Flash Vision**: DeepSeek's official vision API and visual reasoning suite. Empowers developers to execute fine-grained visual document parsing, spatial scene understanding, and multi-image reasoning with high token efficiency.
- **Ornith 1.5 (Ornith AI)**: An end-to-end self-improving foundation model family (397B, 35B, and 9B). Rather than relying solely on static datasets, Ornith 1.5 autonomously generates tasks, designs verification scaffolds, and optimizes its reasoning policies via reinforcement learning.
- **SenseNova U1.5 8B MoT (SenseTime)**: SenseTime's 8B Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) vision-language model, delivering high-throughput visual perception and multimodal reasoning at low compute cost.

## 🎬 Next-Gen Video Synthesis & Editing: Wan 3.0 & Bernini 2
- **Wan 3.0 (Alibaba Tongyi Wanxiang)**: The newest generation of Alibaba's Wan video model family, pushing cinematic video generation with superior temporal consistency, natural physical dynamics, and unified audio-visual synthesis.
- **Bernini 2 (ByteDance)**: ByteDance's upgraded Bernini Diffusers v2 framework, integrating latent semantic planning with diffusion transformers for controllable video generation and camera trajectory steering.
- 🤗 [Bernini Diffusers v2 on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/ByteDance/Bernini-Diffusers-v2)

## 🌍 Interactive World Models & 4D/3D Spatial AI
- **EchoWM (JD Future Academy)**: Enterable Omnimodal World Model (Echo-1.5) capable of generating 720p interactive video, environmental sound effects, music, and speech simultaneously in response to 6-DoF user camera movement.
- **Evoke (Alaya-EVOKE)**: An interactive autoregressive world model featuring an external camera-indexed "world state bank." Enables endless, continuous navigation and live prompt-driven event additions without context drift or memory blowup.
- **4DAnyone**: A state-of-the-art framework for reconstructing photorealistic, animatable 4D human representations directly from single monocular videos.
- **GeoWeaver (Kosmo Research)**: A 3D spatial reconstruction pipeline designed to transform long, unconstrained video sequences into globally consistent 3D geometric environments with drift correction.
- 🔗 [EchoWM Project Page](https://echo-team-joy-future-academy-jd.github.io/Echo-1.5-Page/wm/)
- 🔗 [Evoke World Model Project Page](https://evoke-world.github.io/Evoke/)
- 🔗 [4DAnyone Project Page](https://4danyone.github.io/)
- 🔗 [GeoWeaver 3D Project Page](https://kosmoresearch.github.io/GeoWeaver/)

## 🤖 Embodied AI & Robotics: GEN-1.5 & PaXini
- **GEN-1.5 (Generalist AI)**: An embodied foundation model introducing "physical prompting." Enables robots to learn and perform complex physical manipulation tasks from a single 3-to-12 second sensorimotor demonstration within a 30-second context window—without requiring gradient fine-tuning.
- **PaXini Robot (PaXini Tech)**: Cutting-edge humanoid robotics (TORA series) and multi-axis dexterous hands (DexH series) equipped with 6D Intelligent Tactile Processing Units (ITPU) for high-sensitivity physical manipulation.
- 📖 [Generalist AI GEN-1.5 Blog](https://generalistai.com/blog/gen-1.5)

## 🗣️ Voice AI & Compact TTS: Raon-OpenTTS & Audio 8
- **Raon-OpenTTS 1B (KRAFTON)**: A 1-billion parameter open-source text-to-speech model engineered by gaming studio KRAFTON for expressive, dynamic character voices and real-time interactive game dialogue.
- **Audio 8 TTS Preview (0.1B)**: An ultra-lightweight 100M parameter TTS model designed to deliver fast, natural speech synthesis on resource-constrained edge devices and mobile hardware.
- 🤗 [Raon-OpenTTS 1B on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/KRAFTON/Raon-OpenTTS-1B)
